Privacy Policy: Shift Roster to Calendar

Effective date: 2026-10-14

Shift Roster to Calendar ("the app", package com.sonith.shiftroster) is made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does with it.

Summary

The app keeps your data on your phone. It makes no network calls and does not hold the internet permission. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

Network calls

None. The Android internet permission is explicitly blocked in the app, so there are no hosts it contacts. Text recognition runs on the phone.

What is stored on your phone

  • Roster photos you import (or references to them), the text read from them, and the month and workplace you enter.
  • Your chosen row, the codes read from it, and any corrections you make.
  • Shift types (code, name, start and end time, colour, reminder), your shifts, notes and swap details.
  • App settings.

This is held in the app's private local database. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions and why

  • Camera: to photograph your roster. Optional; you can pick from the gallery instead.
  • Calendar (read and write): to add your shifts as events, to remove the events the app added when you re-import a month, and to list existing events that overlap a shift so you can keep or replace them.
  • Notifications: to show optional shift reminders.
  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker when you choose an image, and accepts images you share to it from other apps.

Calendar and sharing

Events the app creates are stored in the calendar you choose. If that calendar syncs to an online account (for example Google Calendar), the sync is done by your phone and that provider under their own policy. If you share a month as an .ics file, it goes to the app you pick in the share sheet.

Analytics and ads

None.

Deleting your data

In the app you can delete rosters and shifts. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone. Events already in your calendar stay there until you delete them in your calendar app (re-importing a month replaces the events the app added for it).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
