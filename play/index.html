Apps on Google Play: test links

These apps are in closed testing on Google Play; they are not public yet.

Step 1: Join the testers group

Do this first

Google Play only lets members of the testers group open the test links below. Join once, with the Google account you use on Google Play.

Join the Google Group

Use the same Google account you use on Google Play. If it asks for approval, I accept requests (usually within a day).

Can't join?

Email me your Google or Gmail address and I will add you.

Email me to be added

The email opens with a short template: your Gmail address and which apps you would like to test.

About the group: you stay opted in for 14 days, you can leave at any time, and the group is only used to give Play test access. No marketing, and your address is never shared.

Step 2: Open an app's test link

The Join test buttons below only work after Step 1.

  1. 1. Open the test linkOn your Android phone, tap an app's Join test button below. It works once you are in the group and the test is live.
  2. 2. Become a testerTap Become a tester on the Google Play page that opens.
  3. 3. Install and keep itInstall the app from Google Play and keep it installed for 14 days.

Closed testing is how Google Play asks new personal developer accounts to test apps with real people before release. Feedback is very welcome: write to sonythm@gmail.com. You can also read more on the testers page.
