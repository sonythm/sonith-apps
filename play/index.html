Apps on Google Play: test links

These apps are in closed testing on Google Play; they are not public yet.

How to test

  1. 1. Join the groupJoin the Google Group with the Google account you use on Google Play.
  2. 2. Open the test linkOpen an app's test link below on your Android phone and tap Become a tester.
  3. 3. Install and keep itInstall the app from Google Play and keep it installed for 14 days.

Closed testing is how Google Play asks new personal developer accounts to test apps with real people before release. Feedback is very welcome: write to sonythm@gmail.com. You can also read more on the testers page.
