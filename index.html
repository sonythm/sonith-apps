About me

I am a Technical Projects Manager and Architect based in Kerala, India, working across delivery management and architecture. I have worked in software for more than 15 years, moving from project executive roles into project and delivery management, architecture and technical leadership.

I plan and deliver software. I break work into batches, keep several tracks moving and track status honestly. I set product direction and make decisions quickly, and I am comfortable learning unfamiliar platforms such as app stores, cloud consoles, and signing and release processes.

My background is in requirements analysis and Agile methods, across Delphi, the MEAN stack, Xamarin and ASP.NET. I hold a Master of Computer Applications from Bangalore University and a SAFe 5 Agilist certification. I work on industrial automation software using PLCs and C#, including digital twins built with simulators.

I care about privacy and quality. Away from my day job I design and prepare small offline Android apps for everyday jobs, using AI-assisted development and agents: I direct the product, review quality and run the release process. I build these apps on my own time as a solo developer.
