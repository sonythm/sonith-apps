## Full description
(Length: about 3,900 / 4,000 characters)

Home Chef Book is a simple order diary for home bakers and home cooks who take orders from home: cakes, cupcakes, cookies, snacks, sweets, pickles and meals for parties and events. Write down each order in a few taps, see how full every day is, keep track of advances and balances, and know what you really earn.

NEW ORDER IN A FEW TAPS
- Pick a customer, or type a new name and phone and save them in the same step.
- Tap a size to add it: Chocolate cake 1 kg ₹850, Cupcakes box of 6, Murukku 250 g, Mango pickle 500 g jar, Veg biryani per plate. Tap again for two.
- Add details: message on the cake, eggless, less sugar, no onion or garlic, theme.
- Choose the day and time, pick-up or delivery, delivery charge and discount.
- Note the advance (Cash or UPI). The balance is worked out for you.
- Save as a confirmed order, or keep it as an enquiry until the customer decides.

SEE YOUR KITCHEN'S LOAD
- Set how many orders you can handle in a day.
- The calendar shows the next two weeks, day by day, with each day's orders and how full it is.
- You get a warning before you take one order too many. You can still save it.

FROM ENQUIRY TO HANDED OVER
- Enquiry, Confirmed, Preparing, Ready, Handed over: one tap moves an order on, with Undo.
- On hand-over, take the balance in one tap.
- Cancelled? Keep the advance as a cancellation charge, or record the refund.

COST AND PROFIT (OPTIONAL)
- Keep a list of ingredients with your prices: flour, sugar, butter, eggs, cream, cocoa, oil, boxes and more.
- Add a simple recipe to any item to see its estimated cost and margin for every size.
- Each order shows its estimated cost and profit. Starter recipes are included for a few items so you can see how it works.

CUSTOMERS AND KHATA
- Each customer shows all orders, total billed, paid and the balance due.
- Record a payment against an order or on the account.
- Send the order confirmation, the bill or the full account as text on WhatsApp or SMS: "Your order: 1 kg chocolate cake. Delivery Sat 5 pm. Advance ₹500 received, balance ₹350."

READY ON DAY ONE
- A starter menu of 25 home-kitchen items in cakes, bakes, snacks, sweets, pickles and meals, with typical prices and sizes. Change prices, add sizes and your own items, hide what you do not make, set the order.
- When you change a price, old orders keep the price they were made at.
- Expense types for ingredients, packaging, gas and more. Add your own.

SHOPPING LIST
- See what to make tomorrow and, from your recipes, how much of each ingredient to buy. Share the list.

REPORTS
- Day, week, month or year.
- Orders, sales, money collected, still due, expenses, profit and estimated food margin.
- Top items, top customers, busiest days, cash and UPI, expenses by type.
- Tap any number to see the orders or payments behind it.

REMINDERS (OPTIONAL)
- An evening summary of tomorrow's orders.
- A shopping reminder for tomorrow.
- A reminder before each order's hand-over time.
- A follow-up while balances are unpaid. Quiet hours, and each reminder can be turned off.

SHARE AND BACK UP
- CSV for any period, a WhatsApp summary, and a backup file to move to a new phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. A message or file leaves the phone only when you share it yourself.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Data is on one phone only, with no sync. Export a backup regularly, because Android's own backup is switched off for this app.
- Costs and margins are estimates from the prices you enter.
- This app keeps your order book only. It does not handle food licences or registration, labelling or food-safety checks.
- This is not a GST billing app and does not make tax invoices.
- Language: English. Amounts in rupees with Indian grouping (₹1,23,456).
- Version 0.1.0, first release.
