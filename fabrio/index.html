Fabrio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-20 App: Fabrio (com.sonith.fabrio), version 0.1.0 Developer: Sonith Contact: sonythm@gmail.com URL: https://sonythm.github.io/sonith-apps/fabrio/

Short version

Photos of people and skin-tone readings are processed on your phone only and are deleted when you choose. The developer collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no ads. Optional backup and sync go to your own cloud account, not to the developer. Two kinds of download reveal your IP address to the host: AI model files from Hugging Face and the trend catalog from GitHub Pages. Crash reports are off unless you opt in, and never include images. The app is for people aged 13 and over.

What the app stores on your device

  • Fabrics: photos you take or pick, and the colours, pattern, length and notes derived from them.
  • People: names you type, measurements, an optional full-length photo for try-on, AI-model settings, and a skin-tone reading (a colour value used only to match colours).
  • Designs: sketches, choices, notes, collections, tailor details and rates, tailor sheets and PDFs, stitching progress and fit feedback.
  • Settings: language, units, region, theme, text size, sync choices, reminder times.
  • AI models: downloaded model files and, if you add it, the try-on pack.

Data is kept in a local database and in the app's private storage. Photos of people are kept in a separate folder so that "Delete all people photos" removes them.

Photos of people and skin tone

  • Photos of people and the skin-tone reading are processed on the phone. They are not uploaded to the developer or to any AI service.
  • Use only photos of yourself or of someone who has agreed. The app asks you to confirm this before a try-on.
  • Skin tone is used only to suggest matching colours and to shade a drawn figure. It is never used to describe a person's ethnicity or to make decisions about anyone.
  • You can delete one person, or all people photos, in Settings. The skin-tone reading is deleted with the person or in Skin tone settings.
  • AI models and figures created in the app are labelled AI-generated.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer runs no server that receives your fabrics, designs, photos, measurements or skin tone, and holds no copy.

Network calls the app makes

  • Hugging Face: downloading AI model files. Hugging Face sees your IP address and the file requested, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • GitHub Pages: downloading the trend catalog (a small file, checked at most daily). GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Your own cloud (optional): if you switch on backup or sync, data goes to your Google Drive or iCloud account. This is off by default. Photos are included only if you switch on "Include photos".
  • Report a problem / report a design (optional): sent only when you submit one, with what you wrote. Images are never attached automatically. Reports wait on the phone until you are online.
  • Model and catalog downloads use Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data.

Crash reports

Off by default. If you opt in, a report contains the error and device model, never images, names or measurements.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph fabrics and, only if you choose, a full-length photo for try-on. Photos can also be picked from the library instead.
  • Internet / network state: the downloads and optional sync above, and to show online or offline status.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on a trial-fitting or model-update reminder. Scheduled on the phone.
  • Not requested: location, contacts, microphone, broad storage access.

Sharing

Tailor sheets, PDFs, images and exports leave the phone only when you tap Save or Send and pick an app. The receiving app's policy then applies. A tailor sheet contains the design, the measurements and the note you wrote.

Backups

Android's automatic backup is switched off. Use Export designs or your own-cloud backup to move to a new phone.

How to delete your data

  • In the app: delete people, designs, fabrics, or Settings, Delete all people photos.
  • Optional cloud backup: delete the backup folder in your own cloud account.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone.
  • The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing to request from the developer.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over and asks you to confirm your age on first launch. It is not directed at children, and the developer collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
